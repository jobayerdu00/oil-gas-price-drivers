How to read this

Rule of thumb

Every $1 per barrel on crude adds about 2.6 cents per gallon at the pump. A barrel holds 42 gallons, so roughly 100% of the crude cost passes through.

What it leaves out

The model holds the retail spread (refining, taxes, margin) fixed. That spread jumped about 50 cents after 2021, and refinery outages or hurricanes can move it again.

Up vs. down

Prices did not rise measurably faster than they fell at monthly frequency (t-stat about 1), so the same path is used for increases and decreases.

Range

The likely range is a 95% interval on the long-run pass-through estimate (108% ± 19%). It covers model uncertainty, not other shocks.
